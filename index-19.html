🎯 How to play

  1. Move around the 3D house and find the highlighted target animal.
  2. Get close and tap Start Sentence Challenge.
  3. Look at the animal's position and number.
  4. Tap word tiles to build the exact sentence, including the full stop.
  5. Earn 100 points for each correct mission. You can retry if time runs out.
